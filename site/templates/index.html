A cell is one plan in one county. Grades summarize how usable the plan's published behavioral-health roster is in that county, from placeholder contact data, stale attestation dates, shared-only phone numbers, address inflation, and federal registry disagreements. Cells listing fewer than 10 providers are reported as a roster fact instead of graded.

What the files show

  • On average, plans' files attach {{ national.avg_ooa_pct }}% of their provider records to plans whose CMS-filed service area contains none of the record's addresses, nor any adjacent county. These listings cannot serve the plan's geography as published.
  • One issuer's current file carries placeholder phone 999999999 on 85% of records while marking 90% of them as accepting new patients.
  • {{ "{:,}".format(national.deactivated) }} listed provider identifiers appear on the federal registry's deactivated list; half were deactivated more than 20 months before the file was fetched.
  • Several issuers' mandated files have not had a record attestation updated in over a year, against a monthly-update requirement.
