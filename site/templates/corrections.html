How to file one

Email {{ contact }} or open a request on the public correction tracker. Say which finding you mean, and what is wrong about it. A metric name plus an NPI is the most precise way to point at one.

Please do not put anyone's personal or health information in a tracker request, since those are public and permanently indexed. If a correction needs that kind of detail, send it by email instead.

We publish disputes as filed. The one exception is material we will not host, meaning personal or health information, credentials, or abuse. We remove that, and this page says what was removed rather than editing quietly. Every change to a published statement is visible in the file's git history, so "published verbatim" is something you can check rather than something you have to take on faith.

Separately, when a later monthly crawl no longer reproduces a finding, it is marked resolved with a date on the {% if prelaunch %}changes page{% else %}changes page{% endif %}, whether or not anyone disputed it.
